# Money opportunities + keeping the station cheap (researched 2026-11-18)

Numbers below come from public pages on 2026-11-18 and change often. VOLT re-checks before acting.

## A. Side revenue, ranked by fit with RANKED IRL

| # | Idea | Startup cost | Why it fits | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Etsy shop — original fitness/sport/"rank" art** (posters, gym prints, sport line art, rank-tier badges) via print-on-demand (Printful/Printify/Gelato) | ~$0.20/listing + samples | Same audience as the app; every listing links to getcoached.today, so it doubles as marketing | Etsy allows AI-assisted art **with disclosure** and POD **with partner disclosure**; mass-produced near-identical AI designs get flagged. No team names, athletes, brands or characters. |
| 2 | **Printable digital downloads** — workout logs, macro trackers, 12-week planners | ~$0.20/listing | No shipping, pure margin, on-brand | Etsy's 2026 updates tightened AI rules for digital items — you must play a clear creative role. |
| 3 | **Gym-voice stickers/apparel** on Etsy POD + Redbubble | $0 | Your lowercase campaign lines are already merch-ready | Trademark-search every phrase first. |
| 4 | **Affiliate links** in content (phone tripods/stands needed to film sets) | $0 | The app literally needs a phone stand — natural, honest link | Disclose affiliate links (FTC). |
| 5 | **UGC / short-form editing for other fitness apps** | $0 | You already make fitness content for @rickyascension | Don't promote direct competitors. |
| 6 | **Gym ranking events** — "get ranked" nights at local gyms using the app | small | Brings in real users + content | Needs gym permission and waivers. |

About "reselling artwork": reselling art you didn't make, or didn't get a license for, is copyright infringement, and it's the fastest way to get an Etsy shop suspended. Sell your own designs, AI-assisted designs you directed (disclosed), properly licensed art, or verified public-domain works that you've changed in a real way.

## B. Free / cheap ways to run the agents

| Source | What you get | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Claude Code provider in StarNet** | Uses your existing Claude plan | No new bill. Best quality. Plan usage limits apply. |
| **Google AI Studio (Gemini)** | Free tier, Flash/Flash-Lite models | Google no longer publishes fixed free limits; check your AI Studio console. Free-tier data may be used to improve Google products, so keep secrets out. |
| **OpenRouter `:free` models** | 20 req/min; 50/day, or 1,000/day after a one-time $10 credit purchase | Good for daily scans. |
| **Ollama (local)** | Unlimited, offline | Needs a strong GPU/Apple Silicon; weaker on long tasks. |
| **Groq, Cerebras, SambaNova, Cloudflare Workers AI, Cohere** | Free developer tiers | Third-party lists disagree on limits. Check each provider's docs. StarNet can reach OpenAI-compatible ones via the openai-compatible provider. |
| **Startup programs (apply)** | Credits | Anthropic startup program (amount not public); Google for Startups Cloud (reported up to $200k–$350k for AI startups); AWS Activate; Microsoft for Startups; NVIDIA Inception. RANKED IRL is a real app with a live site, so it's a credible applicant. |

Cheapest sensible setup: daily scans on OpenRouter free or Gemini Flash, writing on Claude Code, and apply to Google for Startups + AWS Activate this week.
