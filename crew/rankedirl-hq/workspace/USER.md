## RANKED IRL — product facts (source: Commander's product-marketing.md v1, 2026-09-06)
- **One-liner:** RANKED IRL — the only fitness rank you can't fake.
- **What it does:** iPhone camera counts reps and refuses shallow ones out loud; Apple Watch reads effort; body scan; meals read by camera (photo, barcode, label, voice). Four attributes — Strength, Endurance, Conditioning, Technique — scored against published population norms for age and sex → a rank, a daily mission, a season ladder. Nine sport tracks (golf, basketball, running, tennis, baseball, volleyball, boxing, MMA, wrestling) scored against checkpoints with frame-by-frame replay.
- **Platform:** iOS only (iPhone + Apple Watch). No Android yet.
- **Pricing:** No free tier. 3-day free trial on Standard Yearly. Standard $69.99/yr or $7.99/mo (one sport). Pro $99.99/yr or $14.99/mo (all sports, combat rounds, more scans, replay export). Only offer code: a free month earned at the 5th physique milestone.
- **Audience:** trains 3–5×/week, 18–40, iPhone + Watch, US-first. Personas: the lifter, the recomp athlete, the sport athlete, the competitor.
- **Core pain:** "Fitness apps are expensive notebooks — they write down whatever you type."
- **Differentiation:** verified not typed (camera/watch/scan; footage never uploads, health data stays on phone); composite rank vs published norms; the Results card is the ad; one app replaces a $70+/mo stack; nine sports with replays.
- **Competitors:** Lift AI, Fitnit, REPS, CoachAI (camera counters); Hevy/Strong, Strength Level, MyFitnessPal, Fitbod (all trust what you type); trainers at $60–80/hr; SwingVision/HomeCourt.
- **Proof rules:** Accuracy number is published at getcoached.today/verified only when measured — never quote a number before it is there. No testimonials yet — never invent one.
- **Links:** getcoached.today (install / beta / launch list). Instagram @rickyascension. TikTok.
- **Goals:** trial→paid ≥ 40%, D7 retention ≥ 20% in first 30 days after launch. Key action: install, then first camera set in session one.
- **Brand visuals:** graphite, volt, gold, cream stickers, lowercase campaign lines.
